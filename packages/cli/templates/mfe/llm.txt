## Rules (enforced by pnpm lint)
no raw localStorage/sessionStorage · no @module-federation imports · no window.__PLATFORM_CONFIG__ /
import.meta.env.VITE_PLATFORM_* · no React values across roots · no edits to generated files ·
local kebab-case ids and keys · defaultValue (never value) in settings · literal telemetry event names ·
prefix-relative routes · no MFE-to-MFE imports · no secrets in env.
